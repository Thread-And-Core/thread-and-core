The Problem
A blast contract runs for weeks. The paperwork runs forever.
A single mining contract means a truck to site every day for 30, 40, sometimes 60 days — each with its own delivery, its own docket, its own invoice. Today that lives across three disconnected teams, and none of them see the same picture.
📋

Scheduling on spreadsheets & WhatsApp

Vehicles, crews and blast jobs tracked in Excel and chat — conflicts surface only when the shift starts.

🐢

16 manual steps for one goods receipt

Every delivery takes a long, manual ERP process — posted hours later, so system stock never matches reality.

📄

Paper dockets, lost in the field

Handwritten dockets travel back from remote sites days later — damaged, illegible, or missing — holding up billing.

🙈

Customers flying blind

Mining customers can't see what's scheduled, what arrived, or what they've used — every query is a phone call.

⚡

What if all of it lived on one connected platform?

That's Field Operations Intelligence — one system carrying a delivery from the planner's board, to the operator's tablet in the pit, to the customer's sign-off, to the invoice in your ERP. In real time.

What's Inside
Three connected apps. One source of truth.

Blast Scheduling Board

1000+ SKU Product Catalogue

Offline Mobile Docket

The operator's tablet works fully offline deep in the pit. Captures actual delivered quantities against plan, records variances, and syncs the moment signal returns.
